A Micro-Macro Energy Bridge for Fluid Column Destabilization

Author: Naitik Tyagi • Independent Theoretical Physics Research


This paper examines fluid dynamic models, introducing an equivalent linear elastic representation for bulk modulus and evaluating gravity-driven thinning profiles using geometrically derived, dimensionally consistent relations.

\[ r(h) = \sqrt{\frac{\kappa}{\pi \sqrt{v_0^2 + 2gh}}} \]

Passing dimensional checks (\(\text{kg}\cdot\text{m}/\text{s}^2\)), this analytical model demonstrates geometric thinning toward terminal breakup criteria.
